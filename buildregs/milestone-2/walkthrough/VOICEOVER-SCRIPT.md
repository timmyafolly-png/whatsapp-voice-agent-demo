# Voiceover script

Nine sections, one per slide. Around 70 seconds in total at a normal speaking pace.

## How to record

Record **one continuous take**, in order, and **pause for about two seconds between
sections**. Do not try to match the video's timing — the slides will be re-timed to fit
what you actually said.

Those pauses are the only thing that matters technically: they are how the sections get
split apart. A clear gap of silence, roughly two seconds, then carry on.

Any format is fine — m4a, mp3, wav, or a voice note off a phone. Quiet room, one take,
mistakes and all. If you fluff a line, pause, and say it again; say "again" first so the
bad take is easy to spot and cut.

## What happens after you send it

1. The recording is split on the pauses into nine pieces.
2. Each slide's hold time is set to the length of its piece, so the picture follows the
   voice rather than the other way round.
3. The video is re-rendered at the new timings and the audio muxed in.
4. Out comes a narrated MP4.

If the pause detection makes a mess of it — too quiet a room, pauses too short — the
fallback is nine separate files, one per section, named `01.m4a` through `09.m4a`. That
removes the guesswork entirely.

## A note on the captions

The on-screen captions can stay or go once there is narration. Keeping them helps anyone
watching on mute, which on a phone is most people. Say which you prefer.
